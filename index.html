About

I connect strong science to the capital and strategy it needs to reach patients.

I trained in neuroscience at the University of Michigan and spent four years in research labs, working on synthetic cell signaling, stem cell therapies for ALS, and the genetics of Alzheimer's disease. Before that I worked bedside as a patient care technician on psychiatric and cardiovascular units.

Both taught me the same thing. Whether a therapy or device reaches patients depends as much on markets, reimbursement, and strategy as on the data. I now work on that side of the problem: building market models, commercialization strategy, and investor materials for life science companies, and integrating lenders' business models into enterprise finance software at Alfa.

I'm most interested in roles that bridge research and business, as a scientist inside an early-stage company or on the strategy and investing side of life sciences.
